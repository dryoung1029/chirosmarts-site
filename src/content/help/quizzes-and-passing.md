## Passing score

Quizzes and the final exam have a passing threshold — **80% by default**, though
a course may set its own; the course page and the quiz show the threshold that
applies. You'll see your score immediately after submitting.

## Knowledge checks are required to move on

Each module ends with a **knowledge check**, and the next module stays locked
until you pass it. This is deliberate: watching the videos isn't the whole
course, and your **certificate can't be issued until every knowledge check and
the final exam are passed**. Gating them module-by-module means you find that
out as you go, rather than at the finish line.

If you're stuck on a check, you can retake it as many times as you need — and
after a graded attempt you can jump straight to the video moment covering
anything you missed.

## Retakes

- If you don't pass, you can **try again**. Every attempt is **kept on record** —
  we never erase a failed attempt — but you only need one passing attempt to move
  forward.
- After a graded quiz, you can **jump straight to the video moment** that covers a
  question you missed, so you can review before retrying.

## The final exam

The final exam works the same way, and unlocks only after you've met the
[seat-time and lesson requirements](/help/unlocking-the-final-exam). Passing the
final exam (and any required course quizzes) is what triggers your
**[certificate](/help/your-certificate)**.

Having trouble submitting a quiz? **[Contact support](/help/contact)**.
